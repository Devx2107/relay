# Triage

On load, read recent email and upcoming events, then present roughly 2–5 ranked items. Ranking starts with deterministic signals: urgency, response expectation, sender relevance, deadlines, calendar proximity, recency, and explicit urgency language. Groq may classify ambiguous content, but it is not the authority.

Items need a stable ID, source, summary, reason, urgency, and supported actions. Initial actions are reply, ignore/archive, and snooze where supported. Replies are drafted, editable, and approval-gated before sending.
